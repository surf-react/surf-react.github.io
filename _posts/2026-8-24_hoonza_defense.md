Today's reason for celebration is the successful defence of Honza's Ph.D. thesis titled *Computational studies of phenomena at the level of individual atoms: from single atom catalysts to atom-sized magnets*. The defense took place at the Department of Physical Chemistry, and after the initial presentation a fruitful discussion was held.

We congratulate doctor Honza entering the group as a fresh postdoc and stay tuned for the future science!
