title: "Adam elected chairman of young chemists"
tag: Group news

On August 13 2026, the Young Chemist Association under the Czech Chemical Society held elections for its leadership for the next four-years term. There, Adam was elected the chairman of the association, and together with Michal Zelenský as vice-chair and Tereza Hanusová as treasurer will continue to build a community of young and motivated chemists located int he Czech Republic.
