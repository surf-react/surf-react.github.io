#### Background
Defended his dissertation thesis named *Reaction Mechanisms in on-Surface Synthesis by Computational Chemistry Methods* in 2023 at Palacký University, Olomouc, Czechia. As a computational chemist in the [Nanosurf](https://nanosurf.fzu.cz) lab at Institute of Physics of the Czech Academy of Sciences he authored more than 10 publications in top journals during his PhD studies. In 2019, Prof. Juan Carlos Sancho Garcia hosted Adam for two weeks at the University of Alicante, where he introduced Adam the fractional occupation density calculations in Orca. In 2022, Adam succeeded with his proposal in the open call of the Palacký University Endowment Fund, which allowed him to spend three months in the group of Prof. Hans Lischka at Texas Tech University, Lubbock, TX, USA. There, Adam acquired skills with multiconfigurational and multireference methods. This collaboration resulted in two publications. In 2023, his proposal to the Czech Science Foundation was awarded funding for three years, allowing him to spend two years as a postdoc in the group of Prof. Miklos Kertesz at Georgetown University, Washington, DC, USA. This postdoc fellowship ended in February 2026.
