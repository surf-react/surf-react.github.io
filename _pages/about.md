The idea behind our group, our home, Olomouc, how we started and what is our goal. Don't forget to mention our philosophy for open science, strong ethics, and work/life balance.

The idea to form a young research group studying chemical reactions using computational chemistry methods was in my head for some time by the end of my PhD studies as I contemplated if a postdoc position abroad is for me. After the first year as a postdoc at Georgetown (2025), I asked Jan if he wants to start a group with me after he finishes PhD and I return to Olomouc. This was a natural decision for me as I have always enjoyed discussing science with Jan and I knew that he wants to continue in research. Once he agreed, I started working on a grant proposal for our to be a group. As is often the case, our proposal wasn't funded, so the official launch of a new group was postponed. In 2026, my postdoc finished and Jan is expected to defend his dissertation. Together with our students we continue to study intriguing problems in heterogeneous catalysis and we all grow personally. Despite lacking our first group funding, we decided that it is time to finaly launch.

Therefore, on DD Month 2026, the **Surface Reactions Modelling group** became official!
