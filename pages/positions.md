We are always interested in curious people who want to compute chemistry at surfaces. Even when no call is listed below, feel free to send a CV and a short motivation letter to the PI. We are open to host motivated students for internships and study exchange programs, e.g. through Erasmus.

## M.Sc. and bachelor projects

Topics in adsorption thermodynamics, reaction path finding, method development, and reaction kinetics are available every semester for students of Palacký University. Stop by the department or write to us.
